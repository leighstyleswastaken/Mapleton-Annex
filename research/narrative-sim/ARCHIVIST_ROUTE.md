# Archivist / Whistleblower route checkpoint

## Purpose

This route is the first proof that Mapleton Annex can produce an ending as the final act of a story the player experienced, rather than selecting prose from a late flag check.

It remains research-only. It does not alter the React game, tuner, saves or current story data.

## Player-visible trajectory

1. **Recruitment and interview** establish an ordinary temporary job and ask whether an authorised record can be wrong.
2. **Induction** teaches the existing slider and describes secondary carriers as harmless equipment bleed.
3. **Shadow shift** presents two mundane carriers so dual-band tuning is learned without story pressure.
4. **First anomaly** contradicts the official closure of Employee 49220 with an active Terminal 01 session.
5. **Identification payoff** occurs only when that contradictory carrier was sufficiently recovered: Sana identifies 49220 as Ollie.
6. **Desk-history corroboration** can reveal that Ollie left a recovery key beneath the frequency dial.
7. **Sana investigation** can reveal that live employee sessions are stored under archival labels.
8. **Management consequence** names the exact restricted record the player accessed.
9. **Human corroboration** allows Sana's archived voice to confirm the meaning of the labels.
10. **Mog misdirection** imitates Ollie and attempts to obtain the recovery key.
11. **External line** creates an explicit final choice to transmit evidence or surrender it.
12. **Whistleblower ending** is available only when the player has an identified source, Sana's record, independent corroboration and a visible release window.

## Regression expectations

- Ollie cannot be identified before the active-session anomaly is recovered.
- Management cannot challenge access to Sana's record unless the player recovered it.
- Sana's archived voice cannot count as corroboration unless the carrier was sufficiently decrypted.
- Mog imitation cannot be exposed without prior knowledge of Ollie and the key.
- Whistleblower cannot occur without an explicit release choice.
- Every simulated run must end with a visible outcome, including incomplete or failed routes.

## Current limitations

- Final LOG / CONTAIN outcomes are still simplified; the next pass must distinguish preservation of a carrier from classification of the whole packet.
- Cal's recovery-key bargain is exposed but not yet represented as its own deliberate choice.
- The prose is functional test prose, not final game copy.
- Stress, queue pressure, facility integrity and rank are not yet integrated into this route harness.
- The research simulator approximates tuner behaviour through clarity allocation rather than frame-by-frame slider movement.

## Exit criteria for the research phase

Before a UI prototype begins, this route must:

1. pass its ordering and ending-prerequisite checks across all policies and at least 100 seeds;
2. produce at least one coherent successful transcript and one coherent failed transcript;
3. expose all hidden causal changes in the analyst transcript;
4. contain no route-critical event that changes only an unexplained numeric meter;
5. demonstrate that chasing every secondary carrier is not always optimal;
6. demonstrate that an authorised-carrier run remains coherent even when it misses the mystery.
